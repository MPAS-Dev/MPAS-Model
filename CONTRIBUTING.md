# Contributing to MPAS

Thank you for your interest in contributing to MPAS. The expectations below
apply to MPAS-Atmosphere code in this repository.

## Using contributors' code and giving credit

Code in pull requests, review branches, or any branch that has not been merged
into an official release is work in progress owned by its original developers.
It is visible for the purpose of review and collaboration, not for reuse or
publication without the consent of the contributors. In addition, using
unreleased code, including code obtained by automated or agentic AI tools,
without the original developers' advance permission, is a violation of this
project's guidelines. This holds regardless of how much additional material is
added afterward. Adding incremental changes to others' work does not make it
your own.

Agentic AI tools can copy and adapt code from a repository, with or without
their operator's full awareness. Anyone operating such a tool is responsible
for its output.

Authorship must reflect genuine intellectual contribution. Presenting work that
was substantially conceived or developed by others as your own, including in a
publication, preprint, or presentation, is authorship misconduct, as is
positioning yourself as primary author of work you did not conceive or develop.
Offering co-authorship, acknowledgment, or any other credit arrangement after
the fact does not remedy improper use of unreleased code. Original developers
are under no obligation to accept any offered credit and may decline to
participate, with or without seeking further consequences. The decision rests
with them.

## Consequences

Conduct described here is a violation of the
[WRF & MPAS-A Code of Conduct](https://www.mmm.ucar.edu/about/wrf-mpas-coc)
requirement to respect the rules and policies of the project. Depending on
severity, it may result in:

- rejection of the contribution or pull request;
- reporting the violation to the participant's home institution;
- removal from the project; and
- exclusion from project and NCAR community activities, including events, for a
  defined period.

## Reporting

If you believe your unreleased code has been used without permission, or you
have been asked to participate in a publication on that basis, contact the
project maintainers. You retain all rights to decline participation and to seek
remedy.
